The fine print, in plain words

Shipping, Returns & Privacy

Shipping Returns & Refunds Cancellation Privacy Terms

Shipping & delivery

  • Dispatch: orders are packed and handed to the courier within 1–2 business days of confirmation (orders placed on Sunday ship Monday).
  • Standard delivery: 3–6 business days after dispatch. Current rate: ₹49 per order, free on orders of ₹999 or more (shown live at checkout).
  • Express delivery: 1–2 business days after dispatch, flat ₹99 per order, any order value.
  • Coverage: all serviceable PIN codes across India. Some remote PIN codes may take an extra 1–2 days — we’ll flag it on WhatsApp if yours does.
  • GST invoice is included with every parcel. Prices on the site are GST-inclusive — you never pay anything extra at the door.
  • Undeliverable parcels (wrong address, phone unreachable, refused at the door) return to us; re-shipping is chargeable at the original delivery rate.

Fees and the free-delivery threshold reflect November 2026; checkout always shows the live rate — the checkout page is the source of truth.

Returns & refunds

  • 7-day easy returns: if something arrives damaged, defective or simply not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days of delivery — we’ll replace it or refund you, no drama.
  • How to start: WhatsApp your order number with a photo (or 20-second unboxing video) of the issue. That’s the whole process.
  • Pickup: for damaged/defective items, pickup is arranged by us at no cost. For change-of-mind returns, shipping is on you (same ₹49/₹99 rate).
  • Refunds: issued to the original payment mode where applicable; for COD orders, refunds go out by UPI/bank transfer within 5–7 business days of pickup & inspection.
  • Condition: items must come back unused, with original packaging, accessories and the GST invoice.
  • Not returnable: opened consumables, gift cards, and items marked “final sale” on the product page.

Cancellation

  • You can cancel pending or confirmed orders yourself from My Account → My Orders — stock and any coupon usage are restored instantly.
  • Once an order is shipped, cancellation isn’t possible — it falls under Returns instead.
  • Need help? WhatsApp your order number and we’ll sort it.

Privacy — plain English

  • What we keep: your name, phone, delivery address and order history — only so we can deliver your order, send a GST invoice and help you if something goes wrong.
  • What we don’t do: we don’t sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or brokers. No shady lists, ever.
  • Cookies/storage: your cart and wishlist live in your browser’s local storage; a session cookie keeps you signed in. No cross-site tracking pixels.
  • WhatsApp & email: used to confirm your order, delivery updates and support. You can ask us to delete your data anytime (we keep invoices as required by Indian GST law — purchase records must be retained).
  • Security: passwords are stored hashed (scrypt), never in plain text. Card data is never handled — COD and UPI-on-delivery only.
  • Your rights: ask for a copy or deletion of your data by writing to the email below — expect an honest answer within 48 hours.

Terms of purchase

  • Prices shown are GST-inclusive and may change with stock and supplier costs; the price at the time you order is the price you pay.
  • Stock: products are limited-quantity shelf items. If something sells through during your order, we’ll refund/adjust immediately on WhatsApp.
  • Payment: Cash on Delivery and UPI on delivery. COD may carry a minimum order value and can be disabled during fraud-risk periods — the checkout page shows what’s currently available.
  • Verification: for COD, we may call/WhatsApp to confirm genuine intent before dispatch. Unanswered calls during dispatch attempts may lead to cancellation.
  • Coupons are single-purpose, may carry minimum order values, and can’t be combined with other offers unless stated.
  • Jurisdiction: orders are subject to Indian law; disputes fall under Kolkata, West Bengal courts.

This is a real small business, not a faceless marketplace — if anything above feels unclear, just ask. We’d rather explain than hide behind fine print.
