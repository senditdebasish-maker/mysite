Before you ask…

What happens after I submit?
Your details open in WhatsApp (and land in my leads inbox). I reply personally — usually within minutes — with either a direct answer or a 15-minute call slot. If there’s a fit, you get a fixed quote within 24h.
I’m not in Kolkata — does that matter?

That’s the best starting point. Bring the messy version: “my salon needs more bookings”, “my shop needs to sell online”, “my site looks 10 years old”. We’ll shape it into a plan together on the call — no cost, no pressure.
