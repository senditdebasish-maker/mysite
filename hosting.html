Hosting, demystified

Is the ₹199/yr really all-in?
The plan price is per year + 18% GST. Domain registration (.in first year) is free on annual plans; subsequent renewals at registrar cost (₹450–₹900/yr depending on the TLD — I always quote exact numbers before you commit). No hidden “setup” or “activation” fees.
What does “free migration” involve?
You share your old host’s login (or a backup) — I copy files + databases + mail accounts, test on the new server, then flip DNS. Usually 24–72h, and the site never goes dark during the switch.
What if something breaks at 11 PM?
The Business and Pro plans reach me on WhatsApp any time. Critical outages (site down / mail down) are addressed same-day — that’s the whole point of managed hosting over a ₹99 coupon site.
Can I cancel in month 2?
Yes — 30-day money-back, no questions asked. After that, hosting is prepaid per term; I don’t do lock-in contracts or auto-debit surprises.
